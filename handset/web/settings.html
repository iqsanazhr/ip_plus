Troubleshooting Guide

1. Plug in iPhone via USB and unlock it.

2. Tap Trust This Computer on the iPhone when prompted.

3. Ensure Developer Mode is enabled in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security.

4. Ensure Apple Mobile Device USB Driver is active in Windows Device Manager.
